Ben's Cert Fix

Fixes "cannot connect" on websites and apps by adding the up-to-date certificates old iOS is missing. Open this page in Safari on the iPhone, iPod or iPad. Jailbroken? Install Ben's Cert Fix from Cydia instead.

Install all 27 certificates
  1. Tap the button above and choose Install. iOS calls the profile "Unsigned"; that's normal for profiles that aren't from a company.
  2. iOS 10.3 only: go to Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings and switch on the certificates.
  3. Close and reopen Safari or any app that couldn't connect.

To remove it: Settings → General → Profiles → Ben's Cert Fix → Remove.

Button didn't work? Install them one at a time
